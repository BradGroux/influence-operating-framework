# Glossary

These terms carry specific meaning in the Influence Operating Framework.

## Contribution

A concrete act or artifact intended to create useful value for a person,
community, or ecosystem without making reciprocity a condition.

Avoid using contribution as a euphemism for funnel activity, attention seeking,
or a disguised sales obligation.

## Context

The evidence, history, norms, constraints, relationships, commitments, and
uncertainty needed for a responsible decision.

Context is bounded by purpose and proportionality. It is not an unlimited
intelligence collection.

## Do not contact

An accountable decision that direct outreach should not occur. When the
affected person or their authorized representative set the boundary, only their
later affirmative direction can change it. When the practitioner set an
internal precaution, an accountable practitioner may reconsider it for a
documented reason after reviewing current context and risk.

It is a protective outcome, not a judgment of a person's value.

## Ecosystem

A connected field of people, organizations, communities, events, channels,
projects, needs, and contributions around a shared domain or mission.

An ecosystem is not a market segment or contact list.

## Engagement

Purposeful participation or communication with a person, group, or community.
It includes public participation, collaboration, facilitation, teaching,
speaking, media, introductions, and direct communication.

Engagement is not synonymous with platform interactions or activity volume.

## Evidence

An identified public, community-owned, or authorized source that supports an
observation, interpretation, or decision while leaving uncertainty visible.

## Influence

The earned capacity to help ideas, people, and communities move through useful
contribution, trusted relationships, accountable judgment, and learning.

Influence is not reach alone, follower count, status, access, or control over
others.

## No action needed

A positive decision that the current context requires no new activity. It can
mean that continuity is healthy, the purpose is already served, or another
action would add burden without value.

## Opportunity

A possible way to contribute, learn, collaborate, speak, teach, build, or
create another legitimate outcome. An opportunity is evaluated for fit and
trade-offs; it is not a person or presumed conversion.

## Practitioner

The person or mission-led group applying the framework and remaining
accountable for its judgments, commitments, and external actions.

## Relationship

Truthful continuity and mutual context between people or groups, including
actual interactions, commitments, consent, and boundaries.

A relationship is not a lead stage, score, owned asset, or assumption of
closeness.

## Relevant reach

The extent to which useful work reaches people or communities for whom it is
appropriate and valuable. Relevance, accessibility, and use matter alongside
scale.

## Waiting

A deliberate decision not to act now because timing, capacity, evidence,
consent, or context does not support action. Waiting may include a reason to
review later, but it does not create an automatic retry.
