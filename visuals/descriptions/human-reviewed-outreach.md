# Human-reviewed outreach — accessible description

Research completion leads to identified contribution context and then to a draft that cannot send. A human review has four explicit branches. Approve makes the exact draft eligible for a separately authorized handoff. Revise returns it to drafting and invalidates approval. Wait pauses with a reason. Do not contact creates a durable stop. If a separate system is authorized, only the actual outcome is logged and reflected on. Nothing in the diagram sends a message, and approval alone is not delivery.
