# Contribution before ask — accessible description

A verified community need flows through evidence, consent, and community norms into a set of proportionate contribution options. A human chooses to contribute, collaborate or hand off, wait, or take no action. Contributions and shared ownership both require verification of value, burden, and accessibility. Only then is a separate human question asked: is an ask independently appropriate? A yes leads to human-reviewed outreach; not now or no leads to a safe stop. Contribution never purchases access or reciprocity.
