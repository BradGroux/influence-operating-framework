# 0002: Bounded v1.0.0 supplemental review corrections

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-01
- **Decision owner:** Brad Groux

## Context

Four supplemental reviews tested the initial v1.0.0 release for coherence,
practical application, misuse resistance, and simplicity. They identified
overlapping concerns about material interests, group authority, assisted-work
grammar, terminology, repeated learning guidance, public authority, release
review proportion, validator claims, and completed project residue.

The simplicity review also recommended deleting the seven-move practice method,
all Mermaid diagrams, and most direct-engagement guidance. The application
review found the method useful across three materially different paper
exercises, while the framework's subject is responsible influence and therefore
legitimately includes bounded engagement guidance.

## Decision

Correct v1.0.0 in place with a narrow canonical and repository cleanup:

- require relevant disclosure and management of material interests;
- require legitimate authority for consequential group decisions;
- distinguish accountable human judgment from actions software and AI must not
  perform independently;
- make governance the single public authority statement and keep normative
  meaning in the current canonical framework;
- clarify that the seven moves are one optional traversal of the six concerns;
- normalize decision labels and separate sustainability from commercial
  outcomes within measurement;
- compact repeated learning guidance and make release review proportional;
- describe repository validation according to what it actually checks; and
- remove the completed reset specification and standardize the review record.

Retain the six concerns, seven-move practice method, three inline Mermaid
diagrams, and concise business-level engagement guidance. They clarify the
framework without creating an implementation architecture or technical
conformance layer.

## Consequences

- The accepted material corrections have normative effect through the updated
  canonical documents, not through this record alone.
- The initial supplemental reviews remain visible with their original verdicts,
  severities, limitations, and dissent.
- The maintainer disposition distinguishes accepted findings from rejected or
  downgraded simplicity recommendations.
- The local `v1.0.0` tag moves only after the corrected candidate passes both
  application-oriented and adversarial fixed-point review plus repository
  validation.
