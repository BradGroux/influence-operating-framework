- **Date:** 2026-09-05
- **Authority:** Brad Groux, founding steward, through the current audit and release instruction
- **Predecessor:** Influence v1.0.2; Commons adoption decision 0004 remains historical

Adopt Open Framework Commons v2026.09.05 at annotated tag object

`8868a248457dd7b663563beb243c5ebcbb8ac360`. Authenticated repository API and Git

Later Commons releases still need a separate local decision.

## Content rationale and evidence

The commitments are chosen values, not empirical promises. The existing
privacy, refusal and no-reciprocity rules support these clarifications, while
adverse cases expose interpretations worth narrowing: help made conditional
on contribution, unsafe promises treated as permanent, and reach reported as
causal benefit. Governance now distinguishes normative rationale from effect
claims. A fictional example no longer asserts unmeasured effort savings.

The six concerns, seven optional moves, five outcome dimensions and glossary
retain their structure. Clarifications are substantive where a prior reader
would have imposed contribution debt, continued unsafe work, penalized refusal,
or claimed impact from visibility alone. Such practice must be reconsidered.
These are not editorial changes merely because they match intended values.

Field effectiveness and specialist validation remain absent. Fictional cases
and independent document reviews test interpretation only. No evidence quota
or claim of proven improvement is introduced. Revisit when practitioner
feedback reveals a different decision, unwanted burden, or a supported effect
claim. No substantive dissent has been reported in this decision; historical
dissent remains in its original records.

## Edition policy and alternatives

Use prospective YYYY.MM.DD editions, signed annotated vYYYY.MM.DD tags, and
positive numeric same-day suffixes. Use the actual UTC publication date;
compatibility is described separately. Retaining semantic editions and the
older Commons pin were legitimate alternatives. The owner requested the
calendar transition and independently compatible adoption, making exact
publication identity clearer without assigning meaning to a date.

Do not relabel historical versions, create aliases, move tags, rewrite release
bodies, or update another product. There are no runtime/package/schema versions
or consumers in this repository to migrate. CFF's format version stays 1.2.0;
its product version is a quoted string. The validator and release tooling are
the local version consumers and receive calendar/date regressions.

Relationship's charter at inspected commit
`afa195b576cfd35b219e9d905cd013af6fc265b7` separates continuity/stewardship from
Influence's contribution/participation. Its historical Influence v1.0.0 pin
remains untouched. Influence now makes that boundary discoverable without
requiring the other product or duplicated records.
