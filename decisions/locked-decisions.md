# Locked decisions

**Status:** Accepted initial baseline
**Owner:** Brad Groux
**Effective date:** 2026-08-01

## Authority and principles

The following principles are locked unless amended through [governance](../GOVERNANCE.md):

1. **Leave every community better than you found it.**
2. **Create more leaders, not more followers.**
3. **Value first.**
4. **Relationships over transactions.**
5. **Own the framework, not the tool.**
6. **Growth and monetization are outcomes, not the central goal.**
7. **Public by default, private where ethics, consent, or safety require it.**
8. **Human judgment governs external engagement.**
9. **Every success and failure should improve the system.**
10. **The framework is living, reflective, and anti-fragile.**
11. **The journey belongs to the practitioner; the framework provides the map.**
12. **No generic spam, engagement farming, or deceptive automation.**

## Accepted boundaries

- The framework is not a sales CRM, lead funnel, outreach engine, or public-speaking checklist.
- People are never modeled as leads, prospects, targets, or conversion probabilities.
- Research does not imply engagement. No action needed, wait, and do not contact are valid outcomes.
- No default implementation sends external communication. Human approval is required and remains separate from sending.
- Research must not fabricate facts, roles, relationships, contact data, or evidence.
- Public examples are fictional. Sensitive real-world context belongs in governed private overlays.
- Canonical guidance remains tool-agnostic. Schemas and automation contracts implement it but do not define its meaning independently.
- Growth, reach, speaking, and commercial results are multi-dimensional downstream outcomes, not the central objective.

## Change control

These decisions came directly from the owner build brief and are not routine implementation choices. Proposed changes must use the principle-amendment or ADR process in [governance](../GOVERNANCE.md).
