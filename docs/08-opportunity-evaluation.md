# Opportunity evaluation

An opportunity is a possible way to contribute, learn, collaborate, speak, teach, build, or create a legitimate downstream outcome. Evaluation helps a human make an explainable decision; it does not calculate objective worth.

## Required narrative

Every evaluation states:

- what the opportunity is and who it may serve;
- how it aligns with mission and current goals;
- the evidence used and what remains uncertain;
- expected community value and practitioner value;
- consent, privacy, accessibility, reputation, and ethical risks;
- preparation, travel, money, energy, timing, and opportunity costs;
- potential contribution, artifact, learning, relationship, reach, and commercial outcomes;
- the human decision and why a different reasonable person might decide differently.

## Optional dimensions

Dimensions may include mission alignment, community value, learning, contribution fit, audience relevance, relationship continuity, credibility, reach, accessibility, preparation cost, travel cost, timing, reuse, artifact potential, commercial potential, compounding value, and ethical risk.

Use a small ordinal scale only when comparison helps, such as `low`, `medium`, or `high`. Define what the scale means for the current review. Do not combine dimensions into a universal score, hide uncertainty, or rank people.

## Human decisions

- **Pursue:** commit to a bounded next step.
- **Contribute first:** create useful value before deciding whether an ask fits.
- **Research:** resolve a material uncertainty.
- **Wait:** record a reason and optional review date.
- **Decline:** close the opportunity without negative judgment of people involved.
- **No action needed:** preserve context without a task.
- **Do not contact:** prohibit outreach until a human explicitly changes the restriction with reason.

## Review questions

Would this still be worthwhile without public recognition? Who gains and who carries cost? What evidence would reverse the decision? Does the opportunity create more leaders? Is the timing respectful? What promise will this create? What should be stopped to make room?
