# Ethics, privacy, and safety

Ethics is an operating boundary, not a final checklist.

## Data minimization

Collect the minimum information needed for a declared contribution, continuity, decision, or accountability purpose. Public availability does not automatically make collection, combination, inference, or reuse appropriate.

## Privacy classifications

- **Public:** Intentionally public information suitable for the open repository or a public artifact.
- **Community:** Information intended for a bounded group under its norms; not automatically public.
- **Private:** Authorized context restricted to the practitioner or named collaborators.
- **Sensitive:** Information requiring explicit purpose, least privilege, strong protection, retention limits, and heightened review.

The public repository contains only public framework content, fictional examples, and owner-approved profile statements without contact data.

## Private overlay contract

A private implementation chooses its own tool but must define:

- accountable owner and authorized readers;
- purpose and minimum fields;
- source authority, provenance, and correction path;
- consent and communication preferences;
- access, sharing, retention, deletion, backup, and recovery;
- audit and incident response;
- how public and private IDs connect without exporting private content;
- how do-not-contact and changed consent propagate to every downstream tool.

Private records do not become authoritative because they are searchable, summarized, or generated by an agent.

## Prohibited practices

- private scraping, platform-control bypass, purchased contact lists, or covert enrichment;
- collection of unnecessary sensitive traits;
- fabricated facts, roles, relationships, familiarity, endorsements, or contact details;
- generic spam, mass personalization, urgency, flattery, engagement farming, or deceptive automation;
- autonomous sending or automatic removal of wait and do-not-contact states;
- ranking human value by reach, revenue, role, or access;
- publishing private notes, consent context, or security reports.

## Human stop authority

Any accountable human can stop or narrow work when evidence is weak, consent is absent, community norms are unclear, a recipient may be burdened, privacy classification is uncertain, or expected harm exceeds value. Agents fail closed and surface the reason.

## Corrections and deletion

Correct unsupported claims at their source and preserve enough change history to explain material decisions without retaining harmful content unnecessarily. A valid deletion, consent withdrawal, or do-not-contact request takes precedence over analytical convenience. Do-not-contact changes append an accountable human decision, time, reason, and superseded decision ID rather than replacing history silently. Propagation to indexes, exports, caches, and connected systems is part of completion.

## Professional boundaries

The framework does not establish legal compliance or replace professional, cultural, community, accessibility, safeguarding, clinical, or security judgment. Adopters must apply the laws, policies, and expertise appropriate to their context.
