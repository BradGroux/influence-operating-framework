# Speaking and influence channels

Speaking is one possible contribution channel among workshops, podcasts, newsletters, video, media, community facilitation, open source, mentoring, writing, and direct service.

## Channel fit

Choose a channel by the community's need and the practitioner's mission, evidence, strengths, capacity, accessibility commitments, and willingness to maintain the resulting artifact. Do not assume the largest audience is best.

| Channel | Useful when | Common risk | Contribution-first control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Talk | A tested idea benefits from shared explanation | Visibility substitutes for utility | Publish useful notes, examples, or references. |
| Workshop | Participants need guided practice | Overpromising transformation | State prerequisites, outcomes, limits, and accessible alternatives. |
| Podcast or media | A relevant audience benefits from perspective | Unsupported authority claims | Prepare evidence, disclose interests, and correct mistakes. |
| Newsletter or article | Durable explanation is valuable | Content volume becomes the goal | Publish when there is a concrete lesson or resource. |
| Video | Demonstration improves understanding | Production polish hides weak substance | Pair with inspectable sources and transcript. |
| Community facilitation | Others need space and structure | Practitioner becomes the center | Share authority and create more facilitators. |
| Open source | A reusable artifact solves a real need | Maintenance burden is externalized | Define ownership, contribution path, and lifecycle. |
| Mentoring | A person requests relevant support | Power imbalance or dependency | Obtain consent, set boundaries, and encourage autonomy. |

## Modular assets

A practitioner may adapt one evidence-backed idea into short talks, full sessions, workshops, guides, demos, or discussions. Each adaptation still needs audience fit, a clear outcome, evidence, accessibility, and honest limits. Reuse should reduce waste, not produce generic submissions.

## Opportunity records

Speaking and media opportunities use the same [opportunity evaluation](08-opportunity-evaluation.md) as other contributions. Paid work is legitimate but remains one dimension. Record conflicts of interest, sponsor constraints, recording and reuse rights, accessibility, travel and preparation cost, and whether the community can safely decline.
