# Research methodology

Research exists to support respectful understanding and defensible decisions, not to maximize collected intelligence.

## Claim types

- **Fact:** A statement directly supported by cited public or authorized evidence.
- **Inference:** A reasoned interpretation derived from identified facts; alternatives remain possible.
- **Hypothesis:** A question or tentative explanation awaiting evidence.

Confidence describes support, not importance: `low`, `medium`, or `high`, with narrative reason. Unknown is valid.

## Source hierarchy

Prefer current primary sources such as an organizer's site, official project documentation, an individual's own public profile, or a directly authorized record. Use reputable secondary sources when they add independent reporting or history. Aggregators, snippets, and agent summaries are discovery aids, not final evidence when a primary source is available.

## Research procedure

1. State the decision or map question and the minimum evidence needed.
2. Check existing authoritative records before searching.
3. Define public, community, private, and sensitive boundaries.
4. Collect the minimum necessary sources and record URL, title, publisher, access date, publication date when known, and a concise claim summary.
5. Label each supported claim fact, inference, or hypothesis.
6. Verify consequential current roles, dates, event details, consent, and communication restrictions.
7. Preserve contradictory evidence as separate records and explain the conflict.
8. Assign confidence and a review or stale-after date proportional to volatility.
9. Have a human decide whether evidence supports mapping, contribution, engagement, more research, wait, no action, or do not contact.
10. Record corrections and outcomes so future research improves.

## Staleness

Volatile claims such as current roles, event schedules, deadlines, communication preferences, and organizational policy require short review windows. Durable historical facts may use longer windows. A stale claim is not automatically false, but it cannot support consequential outreach or travel without review.

## Contradiction

Do not overwrite an inconvenient source. Record what differs, source authority, dates, likely scope differences, and the human disposition. If the conflict cannot be resolved proportionately, lower confidence or stop.

## Privacy and proportionality

Do not collect private contact details, family information, precise location, sensitive traits, or inferred vulnerabilities for influence work. Public availability is not blanket permission. Store authorized private context only in the governed overlay and only for a declared purpose.

## Automation limits

An agent may gather approved public sources, extract candidate claims, find duplicates, flag missing dates, and surface contradictions. It may not fabricate missing fields, infer private attributes, bypass access controls, claim verification without a source, or decide to contact someone.

## Review queue

Every uncertain, stale, conflicting, sensitive, or consequential claim enters a human review queue with source links and a recommended safe disposition. A queue item can close as verified, corrected, rejected, wait, no action, do not contact, or insufficient evidence.
