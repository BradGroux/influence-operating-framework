# Relationship intelligence

Relationship intelligence preserves truthful continuity: how context was discovered, what actually happened, what value was exchanged, what was promised, and what boundaries apply.

## What a relationship record means

A relationship links two participants and describes supported context. It may represent community membership, collaboration, mentorship, introduction, professional familiarity, or another explicit type. A relationship record is not proof of closeness, endorsement, consent to contact, or commercial interest.

See the [relationship graph model](../visuals/exports/svg/relationship-graph-model.svg) and [accessible description](../visuals/descriptions/relationship-graph-model.md).

## Required practice

- Record how the practitioner discovered the context.
- Link public or authorized evidence.
- Separate shared interests from inferred similarity.
- Record actual interactions and contributions by ID.
- Preserve promises, responsible owner, due state, and completion evidence.
- Store communication preferences only when authorized and necessary.
- Use qualitative health states—unknown, new, active, quiet, needs-repair, closed—supported by notes and evidence.
- Record last meaningful interaction without equating recency with value.
- Allow next thoughtful action, no action needed, wait, and do not contact.

## Dispositions and precedence

`do_not_contact` is a durable human restriction and overrides recommendations. Every restriction and disposition is an append-only human decision with a stable decision ID, accountable person ID, time, reason, and link to the decision it supersedes. `wait` includes a reason and optional review date. `no_action_needed` is a positive conclusion that continuity is healthy without another task. An agent may recommend these outcomes but may not remove or weaken them.

## Edge cases

- **Same event, no interaction:** Record event co-presence only if useful; do not create a personal relationship.
- **Public reply or like:** Record an interaction only when context matters; do not infer familiarity.
- **Introduction promised:** Track the promise separately and obtain consent from both sides before making it.
- **Silence after outreach:** Do not label rejection or schedule repeated contact automatically. A human chooses wait, no action, or do not contact.
- **Conflicting recollections:** Preserve both authorized accounts and lower confidence; do not manufacture consensus.
- **Changed role:** Keep historical evidence and mark the former claim stale rather than rewriting history.

## Minimum-necessary retention

Keep only context needed for respectful continuity or accountable commitments. Private notes must have purpose, access, retention, correction, and deletion rules in the practitioner's private overlay. Public records must not contain personal contact details or sensitive observations.
