# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Participation in the Influence Operating Framework community should make the
framework clearer, safer, more useful, and easier to trust. It should create
more capable contributors and leaders, not concentrate status or pressure
people into engagement.

## Expected Conduct

Participants must:

- be specific, evidence-based, and open to correction;
- respect consent, privacy, boundaries, names, pronouns, cultures, and lived
  experience;
- critique ideas and artifacts without demeaning people;
- make space for quieter contributors and explain context without gatekeeping;
- disclose conflicts of interest and commercial incentives that could shape a
  recommendation; and
- follow maintainer instructions used to contain harm or restore productive
  participation.

## Unacceptable Conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxing, unwanted contact, deceptive
identity claims, manipulative outreach, retaliation, sustained disruption, and
publishing private or confidential information are unacceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
release activity, and other spaces where someone represents the framework or
its community.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report a concern privately to the founding steward through a private contact
route listed on the [`@BradGroux`](https://github.com/BradGroux) GitHub profile.
If no suitable private route is available, use the
[private conduct contact request](https://github.com/BradGroux/influence-operating-framework/issues/new?template=private-conduct-contact.yml)
without sensitive details. The public request is not a private reporting
channel; it asks the steward to establish one.

The steward may correct or remove content, limit participation, close a
discussion, or block a participant when necessary. Decisions should state the
reason and remain proportionate to the conduct and risk. If the steward is
materially involved, an uninvolved reviewer should participate when practical,
and any governance limitation must be recorded.
