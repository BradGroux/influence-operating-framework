# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a respectful, accessible, and harassment-free community. Participation should create more capable contributors and leaders, not concentrate status or pressure people into engagement.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, evidence-based, and open to correction.
- Respect consent, privacy, boundaries, names, pronouns, cultures, and lived experience.
- Critique ideas and artifacts without demeaning people.
- Make space for quieter contributors and explain context without gatekeeping.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and commercial incentives that could shape a recommendation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, unwanted contact, deceptive identity claims, manipulative outreach, retaliation, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Until a private reporting channel is published, do not place sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Contact the repository owner through an already trusted private channel. The owner must document outcomes without exposing reporters or affected people. This channel is a declared release deferral, not a completed safeguard.
