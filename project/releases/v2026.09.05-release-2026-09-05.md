# Influence Operating Framework v2026.09.05

This documentation edition follows v1.0.2. It adopts Open Framework Commons
v2026.09.05 independently at `8868a248457dd7b663beb243c5ebcbb8ac360`.
The product edition and shared adoption pin remain separate decisions.

## Changes and compatibility

- Help, clarification, accommodation and support need not be earned through contribution.
- Refusal must be viable without loss of unrelated benefits or safety; unsafe commitments can end within consent and authority.
- Measurement distinguishes completion, use, reported benefit and causal impact. Feedback and continued contact are not owed.
- Governance separates chosen commitments from effect claims and bounds conflict pauses to affected work.
- The fictional assisted-contribution example no longer claims unmeasured effort savings.
- The independent Relationship product remains optional; no combined pipeline or duplicate records are required.
- Calendar-aware metadata and candidate-history checks, signed immutable release procedures, and regression coverage strengthen repository assurance.

These are substantive clarifications for readers who inferred contribution
debt, involuntary continuity or impact from visibility. Reconsider those
interpretations before applying the edition. The six concerns, seven optional
moves and five outcome dimensions retain their structure. No runtime,
implementation contract or automatic downstream adoption is introduced.

## Edition transition and history

New editions use the UTC publication date, with .1, .2 for further releases
on the same day. Order by date, then numeric correction; v1.0.2 precedes this
first dated edition. Dates do not promise compatibility or effectiveness.
Historical tags, releases, citations, reviews, decisions and adoption pins
remain unchanged. There are no dated aliases for older versions.

## Verification and limits

See the [audit disposition](https://github.com/BradGroux/influence-operating-framework/blob/v2026.09.05/project/reviews/calendar-edition-disposition-2026-09-05.md)

No downloadable assets are required for this documentation edition.

Examples and scenario reviews are fictional, not field or specialist evidence.
No effectiveness, accessibility certification, practical adoption or causal
benefit is established by this release. Mermaid's direct renderer is pinned;
its transient dependency resolution is not fully reproducible. Older unsigned
v1.0.0/v1.0.1 releases retain that historical limitation; v1.0.2 signature
verification remains available at its original checkout.
