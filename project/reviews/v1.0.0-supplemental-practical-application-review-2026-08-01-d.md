## Executive summary

Three original paper exercises reached responsible, explainable decisions using
ordinary prose. The framework exposed purpose, beneficiaries, evidence,
uncertainty, contribution, relationships, options, accountability, stop
conditions, outcomes, and bounded learning without a schema, template, score,
workflow engine, or specialist interpretation.

No Blocker or Material problem was found. Two terminology issues create
avoidable friction but did not change the likely decisions.

## Application exercises

### 1. Contribution without an existing relationship

An outside grant writer responded to a volunteer group's public request for help
interpreting funding rules. The framework supported **contribute first**, kept
the absence of a relationship explicit, limited research to current public
sources, and treated a moderator redirect as useful evidence rather than
failure. The result was a bounded addition to an existing community-owned guide.

### 2. Mission-led partnership with unequal power

A community oral-history trust considered a large foundation offer requesting
exclusive rights, product training, and launch control. The framework supported
**revise**, protected storyteller consent and community ownership, and made
declining valid if the sponsor would not accept bounded terms. A narrower grant
was accepted only after rights and capacity concerns were addressed.

### 3. Paid reach versus existing commitments

An independent facilitator considered a paid vendor course that conflicted with
existing community promises and requested rights she did not possess. The
framework supported **revise**, followed by **decline** when timing and
exclusivity remained unacceptable. Smaller relevant reach and fulfilled
commitments were not treated as failure merely because revenue was forgone.

### Minor

#### N-01: Canonical decision labels drift across documents

The operating framework uses `proceed`, `no action needed`, and `do not contact`,
while the method uses `pursue`, `take no action`, and `not contact`. Use one set
of decision labels throughout.

#### N-02: Sustainability and commercial outcomes share one heading

The combined heading can imply that money offsets workload or community burden.
State explicitly that operational sustainability and commercial results are
assessed separately within the dimension and cannot be netted against one
another.
