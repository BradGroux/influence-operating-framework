## Executive summary

The candidate is a coherent, human-readable business operating framework. The
charter establishes its mission and boundary; six concerns define the substance
of responsible practice; seven non-mandatory moves make it usable; the
responsible-practice standard supplies minimum conduct; and measurement closes
the learning loop without a universal influence score.

An individual or mission-led group can apply it through ordinary written
reasoning and a named decision owner. No schema, database, score, automation
layer, or specialist tool is required. One example-level evidence omission does
not prevent release.

## Application evidence

Two fictional paper exercises tested the framework beyond the checked-in
examples:

1. An individual considered a requested introduction while separating actual
   relationship history, consent, burden, and possible no-action outcomes.
2. A mission-led group considered conditional sponsorship while addressing
   purpose, power, ownership, community benefit, trade-offs, and a decision to
   revise or decline.

Both exercises produced an explainable decision, stop conditions, and a bounded
learning response without hidden implementation machinery.
