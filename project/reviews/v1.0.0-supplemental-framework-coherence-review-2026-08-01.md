## Executive summary

The candidate is a business operating framework rather than a technical
specification, outreach funnel, personal-brand system, or checklist presented as
governance. Its six concerns provide a stable decision model; the seven moves
make it usable; the standard provides meaningful boundaries; and measurement
supports learning without a universal score.

Two business-logic omissions prevent a GO: material interests can remain
undisclosed, and a person acting for a mission-led group need not possess
authority for the consequential decision or commitment they own.

## Findings

### Blocker

None.

### Material

#### M-01: Material interests and conflicts can remain undisclosed

**Evidence:** `framework/operating-framework.md:45-47` and
`framework/measurement-and-learning.md:46-53` at the reviewed commit.

The framework permits commercial work and calls for transparent commercial
outcomes, but it does not require disclosure or management of financial,
employment, sponsorship, referral, organizational, or similar interests that
could change how affected people interpret the work.

Required resolution: require identification, relevant disclosure, and
proportionate management through independent review, recusal, narrowing, or
declining. Disclosure alone must not cure coercion, missing consent, or
displaced authority.

#### M-02: Named group accountability does not establish legitimate authority

**Evidence:** `framework/operating-framework.md:126-132` at the reviewed
commit.

The framework requires a named decision owner but does not require that person
to hold a mandate covering the decision, commitment, or external action. A
person can therefore appear accountable while acting beyond their authority or
binding affected people who did not authorize them.

Required resolution: require a legitimate mandate for consequential group
decisions and escalation or narrowing when the named owner lacks it.

### Minor

#### N-01: Practice-method diagram implies a required sequence

**Evidence:** `framework/practice-method.md:3-28` at the reviewed commit.

The prose says the seven moves may overlap and repeat, while the directional
cycle reads as a lifecycle. Explicitly state that the arrows show one optional
traversal and that any move may be revisited.

#### N-02: Glossary weakens the six-concern distinction

**Evidence:** `framework/glossary.md:13-19` and
`framework/glossary.md:76-84` at the reviewed commit.

The Context definition absorbs relationships and commitments, while Purpose,
Judgment, and Learning lack glossary entries. Clarify the boundary between
Context and Relationship and define all six concerns.

### Suggestions

#### S-01: Make purpose effectiveness explicit in reflection

**Evidence:** `framework/measurement-and-learning.md:66-84` at the reviewed
commit.

Ask directly whether the declared purpose advanced so that activity, reach, or
commercial return cannot stand in for purpose.
