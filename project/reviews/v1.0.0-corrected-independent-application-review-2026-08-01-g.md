## Application evidence

1. **Individual contribution without an existing relationship:** The framework
   supported a bounded public contribution based on an evidenced need without
   implying familiarity, reciprocity, or private outreach. The decision was
   **contribute first**, with explicit stop conditions.
2. **Mission-led group with authority and a material interest:** The group could
   identify and disclose the decision owner's sponsor or employment interest,
   use independent review or recusal, and require an authorized board member to
   approve or escalate the commitment.
3. **Assisted external-action preparation:** Software or AI could prepare
   research and a draft, while an accountable person had to review the specific
   context, boundaries, and exact action. Batch or perfunctory approval did not
   qualify as accountable review.

The exercises also kept sustainability and commercial outcomes separate and
used the canonical decision labels without hidden implementation machinery.
