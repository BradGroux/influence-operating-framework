## Executive assessment

The candidate is a coherent, human-readable business operating framework. The
charter sets mission, scope, commitments, exclusions, and accountability; the
six concerns define what responsible practice must make clear; the seven moves
provide a usable, non-funnel method; the responsible-practice standard supplies
minimum boundaries; and measurement and learning close the loop without a
universal influence score. The glossary consistently rejects sales-funnel,
personal-branding, and relationship-scoring interpretations.

An individual practitioner and a mission-led group can each apply the framework
with ordinary written answers and a named decision owner. Neither needs a
schema, database, automation layer, or specialized tool. The authority boundary
is also clean: canonical requirements live under `framework/`; decision records
explain accepted rationale; examples and project records remain subordinate
(`framework/README.md:32-39`; `GOVERNANCE.md:13-23`). This is comparable to the
structural clarity of the read-only AI-Native Operating Framework benchmark
without importing its domain concepts.

No Blocker or Material findings remain. One Minor example-level omission does
not prevent release.

## Framework coherence

The framework components reinforce rather than compete with one another:

- The charter defines influence as earned capacity practiced with others and
  makes contribution, relationships, judgment, and learning constitutive rather
  than promotional (`framework/charter.md:7-20`). It explicitly serves both
  individuals and mission-led groups and keeps responsibility with the
  practitioner despite assistance (`framework/charter.md:22-30`).
- The six concerns are questions, not stages or records, and can be used in any
  order (`framework/operating-framework.md:3-9`). Their completion test is a
  responsible decision, not exhaustive collection or metric production
  (`framework/operating-framework.md:160-170`).
- The seven moves translate those concerns into practice while allowing overlap,
  repetition, pauses, and endings without engagement
  (`framework/practice-method.md:3-10`). The method gives explicit checkpoints
  and makes waiting, declining, no action, and not contacting someone successful
  completion states (`framework/practice-method.md:164-174`).
- Evidence categories and proportional-research questions keep uncertainty
  visible and limit unnecessary collection (`framework/responsible-practice-standard.md:7-44`).
  Consent and communication boundaries override an engagement opportunity, and
  do-not-contact can be changed only by accountable human judgment
  (`framework/responsible-practice-standard.md:61-77`).
- The five outcome dimensions preserve contribution, relationship, learning,
  relevant reach, and sustainability/commercial effects as distinct readings;
  the framework expressly rejects a universal combined score
  (`framework/measurement-and-learning.md:6-64`). Reflection and maintenance
  then turn bounded evidence into proportionate, reviewable change
  (`framework/measurement-and-learning.md:66-100`).

The scope remains a business method throughout. Technical assistance is allowed
but cannot decide whether to contact someone, authorize risk, or redefine the
framework (`framework/operating-framework.md:117-137`;
`framework/responsible-practice-standard.md:79-98`). Commercial outcomes are
acknowledged but subordinated to contribution, consent, purpose, and sustainable
capacity rather than treated as the center of influence
(`framework/charter.md:44-45`; `framework/measurement-and-learning.md:46-53`).

## Fictional application probes

### Probe 1: individual practitioner and a requested introduction

Fictional scenario: Theo, an independent mentor, is asked by a learner for an
introduction to a former colleague. Theo has not spoken with that colleague for
three years and holds a private email address from their old work together.

Application result:

- **Orient / Purpose:** Help the learner pursue a relevant apprenticeship
  without overstating Theo's relationship or spending another person's
  attention.
- **Understand / Context and Relationship:** Treat the past working relationship
  as real but stale; do not share the private address; distinguish the learner's
  request from evidence that the former colleague welcomes introductions.
- **Choose / Judgment:** Theo remains the decision owner. He declines the
  immediate introduction and offers to review the learner's public application.
  He chooses no contact with the former colleague unless a current, truthful,
  appropriately consented reason emerges.
- **Contribute, Sustain, Learn:** Deliver the bounded review, record no
  unnecessary personal context, and assess whether the learner received useful
  help without treating an introduction, reply, or new relationship as the
  success measure.

The framework produces a clear, proportionate decision using a short written
note. It protects privacy, avoids fabricated continuity, supports a useful
alternative contribution, and normalizes no contact without hidden machinery.

### Probe 2: mission-led group and conditional sponsorship

Fictional scenario: Harbor Arts Cooperative is offered funding for free youth
workshops, but the sponsor asks to reuse participant photographs and contact
former attendees directly. Existing participant permissions cover the
cooperative's program reporting, not sponsor promotion.

Application result:

- **Orient / Purpose:** Keep youth access and participant safety ahead of the
  funding opportunity; state that losing the sponsorship is an acceptable stop
  outcome.
- **Understand / Context:** Review the actual offer and current permissions,
  mark sponsor reuse as unauthorized, and avoid inferring consent from prior
  participation.
- **Choose / Judgment:** Name the program director as decision owner. Offer a
  lower-burden alternative such as aggregated program results or a separately
  opt-in public showcase; decline the sponsorship if direct access remains a
  condition.
- **Contribute, Engage, Sustain:** Communicate the boundary through the official
  sponsor channel, make no participant list available, honor existing promises,
  and record only what the cooperative needs for the decision and follow-through.
- **Learn and measure:** Review participant benefit, respected boundaries,
  workload, relevant reach, and foregone or retained funding separately. Do not
  let revenue erase relationship or consent costs.

The group can apply the method with a named human owner, a bounded decision
record, and its existing communication process. The probe requires accountable
governance but no framework-mandated technology or data structure.

## Examples assessment

The three examples are visibly fictional, state that they are illustrative and
not community-validated, and disclaim any prescriptive force
(`examples/README.md:3-10`). They demonstrate contribution before recognition,
truthful but limited relationship continuity, no-action completion, and a
do-not-contact boundary while tracing back to canonical documents. Their roles,
formats, and scenario details do not create framework requirements.

### Minor

1. **The post-decline artifact in the no-contact example has thinner need
   evidence than the canonical contribution standard.** After the proposal is
   declined, Mara infers a resource gap by comparing the accepted agenda and
   publishes a standalone guide (`examples/03-no-contact-decision.md:53-61`).
   The canonical framework says contribution begins with an evidenced need
   (`framework/operating-framework.md:71-92`) and asks whether a community
   requested or validated that need
   (`framework/responsible-practice-standard.md:100-117`). An agenda omission
   alone does not establish need. This is Minor because the canonical rule is
   clear and the issue is confined to one illustrative outcome. A future
   clarification should add a community signal validating the narrower need,
   frame the guide as a bounded hypothesis open to correction, or end with no
   additional artifact.

## Limitations

This was a source-bounded application and consistency review of the exact
candidate, supported by two fictional paper probes. It did not exercise a live
organization, interview affected communities, or test an implementation. It
does not establish legal, ethical, privacy, accessibility, safeguarding,
professional, or domain certification. Passing repository checks establishes
document integrity only.
