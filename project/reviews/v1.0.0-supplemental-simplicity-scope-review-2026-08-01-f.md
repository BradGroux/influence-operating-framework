## Executive summary

The candidate is substantially simpler and more appropriate than the superseded
technical toolkit. No mandatory schema, API, record format, software
architecture, or conformance layer remains. The reviewer nevertheless found
overlap between the six concerns and seven moves, split public authority,
disproportionate outreach detail, and a grammatical contradiction in the rules
for people and tools.

## Findings

### Blocker

None.

### Material

#### M-01: Six concerns and seven moves appear to be overlapping core models

The concerns are non-sequential, but the separate method adds seven named moves,
a directional cycle, checkpoints, completion rules, and scale variants. Most
moves map closely to a concern, and the examples primarily use the concerns.

Reviewer recommendation: remove the standalone practice method, preserve only
unique stopping guidance in the operating framework, and leave scenario
sequences to examples.

#### M-02: Public canonical authority is split

The framework directory is described as canonical while governance places
accepted decisions above most canonical documents, and repository instructions
repeat the hierarchy. Readers cannot know whether current meaning lives in the
canonical body or an unincorporated decision record.

Required resolution: make governance the single authority statement, keep
normative meaning in the charter and current framework documents, and treat
decision records as rationale that authorizes corresponding canonical edits.

#### M-03: Detailed outreach policy becomes a second canonical subject

Consent and accountable external action belong in an influence framework, but
recipient-origin mechanics, legal-duty exceptions, repeated retry rules, and a
full pre-send checklist read like a universal outreach procedure.

Reviewer recommendation: retain proportionate research, truthful relationships,
explicit boundaries, silence as no consent, and accountable external action;
move detailed outreach procedure out of the canonical core.

#### M-04: Assisted-work grammar prohibits the human decision it requires

The standard makes people, software, and AI the subject and then says “They may
not” decide that a person should be contacted or send externally. Grammatically,
the rule prohibits accountable people from making the decision that the charter
and Judgment concern assign to them.

Required resolution: separate human accountability from actions software and AI
must not perform independently.

### Minor

#### N-01: Learning is repeated across several process models

The Learning concern, Learn move, seven-step reflection, and five-step
maintenance sequence substantially overlap. Compact the repetitions while
retaining the distinct five outcome dimensions.

#### N-02: Every release requires two heavyweight reviews

Requiring both independent reviews for a typo or repository-only patch is
disproportionate. Reserve both for material canonical releases and use
proportionate review plus document checks for lower-risk changes.

#### N-03: Validation claims more than it checks

The README says validation proves internal consistency, while the script checks
document structure, versions, links, fences, Mermaid declarations, and removed
paths. It also reports external links as inspected although it does not fetch
them. Describe the result as structural checks, distinguish validated local
targets, and disclose unfetched external links.

### Suggestions

#### S-01: Remove the diagrams and Mermaid renderer

The reviewer considered all three diagrams redundant with adjacent prose and
recommended deleting them with the renderer and Mermaid validation logic.

#### S-02: Remove the completed reset specification

The implementation plan is tied to a completed fixed point. Its durable
framework boundary and rationale already live in decision 0001 and the
changelog, while Git preserves history.

## Editorial notes not counted as findings

- “Working definitions” may read more naturally than “approved vocabulary.”
- Repeated negative boundary statements can overshadow the positive method.
- Repeated six-concern headings in examples are acceptable because examples are
  explicitly illustrative.

## Verification

- Repository document validation passed at the reviewed commit.
- Mermaid validation passed.
- Repository size and canonical overlap were inspected directly.

## Limitations

The severity of M-01 and M-03 depends on editorial judgment about how much
method and engagement guidance an influence framework should contain. The
review records the strongest simplicity case; final disposition belongs to the
maintainer and should preserve any rejected dissent.

## Verdict

**NO-GO.** The reviewer recommends resolving the four Material findings before
re-establishing v1.0.0.
