## Fictional adopter scenario

### Scenario boundary

Amina Rahman is a fictional community accessibility educator evaluating the fictional Maple Harbor Inclusive Teaching Exchange.

Her mission is to help volunteer educators make events and teaching materials usable by people with varied access needs. The bounded cycle allows four volunteer hours per week and stops if organizer authority, accessibility-review capacity, or event access details cannot be confirmed.

No commercial outcome, endorsement, invitation, or response is expected.

### Lifecycle trace

| Stage | Application | Accountable result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discover | Notice a public organizer request for a plain-language facilitator checklist. | Human approves one bounded research question, not a contact list. |
| Research | Create separate fictional evidence records for event logistics, the expressed need, Sam Ortega's organizer role, and the actual planning interaction. | Each material claim has its own source, date, claim type, confidence, and freshness. |
| Verify | Dates and hybrid format are supported. Captioning, quiet-room, and step-free access information remain unknown. | Use `{ "state": "unknown", "reason": "..." }`; no placeholder or invented detail. |
| Map | Link Amina, Sam, the organizer, event, opportunity, interaction, and evidence only as needed for the checklist decision. | No inferred closeness from role or event participation. |
| Prioritize | Compare checklist contribution, broader event participation, further research, and no action. | Human chooses `contribute_first`; outreach stays subordinate to the contribution. |
| Contribute | Plan an HTML and plain-text session-preparation checklist with community review, maintenance ownership, accessibility review, and explicit stop conditions. | Contribution remains planned until review and completion evidence exist. |
| Engage | The recorded interaction says Sam invited one draft through the community platform. | Amina reviews one exact draft; approval binds draft, recipient, content hash, channel, and expiry. No send occurs. |
| Follow up | Record Amina's promise to prepare the checklist and its due date. | Keep the commitment open until evidence demonstrates fulfillment. |
| Reflect | Compare the expected review-ready checklist with actual review effort, accessibility feedback, beneficiary value, and volunteer burden. | Separate observations from interpretation and retain null or adverse outcomes. |
| Improve | Narrow unclear sections and require access details earlier in future event evaluations. | Profile-local, reversible change only. |
| Repeat | Human decides whether another bounded cycle is justified. | Repeat, pause pending access information, or stop are all valid. |

## Detailed findings

### APP-BLK-001 — Relationship do-not-contact is bypassable without a contribution edge

**Evidence**

- `docs/04-relationship-intelligence.md:23-25` says do-not-contact is durable and overrides recommendations.
- `workflows/human-reviewed-outreach.md:24-26` requires an active restriction to override a conflicting draft.
- `scripts/validate.py:240-253` considers a relationship only when both the recipient participates and the draft's contribution ID appears in `relationship.contribution_ids`.
- `schemas/outreach-draft.schema.json:9-17` provides no explicit relationship reference.
- `project/reviews/v1.0.0-rc.1-code-review-2026-08-01.md:23-30` records linked relationship precedence as fixed.

**Reproduction**

In a disposable copy, change the current Lina-Jules relationship disposition to `do_not_contact`, leave the relationship referenced by the recipient person, clear only `relationship.contribution_ids`, approve the exact outreach draft as Lina with a valid scope, and run the schemas gate.

```text
EXIT: 0
PASS schemas
PASS graph-references
```

**Impact**

A stale, incomplete, or deliberately omitted contribution edge makes a relevant relationship restriction invisible. An approved portable draft can therefore coexist with a durable do-not-contact decision.

**Smallest acceptance condition**

Bind outreach explicitly to the relationships reviewed, or derive the relevant practitioner-recipient relationship independently of `relationship.contribution_ids`. Reject approval when any relevant current relationship is do-not-contact. Add the exact missing-edge regression test.

### APP-MAT-001 — Completion state is not bound to accountability invariants

Completed contributions passed with pending accessibility and empty completion evidence. Completed commitments passed with `due_state: overdue`.

**Impact:** A portable implementation can declare work complete while review is pending, no completion evidence exists, or the same commitment is simultaneously complete and overdue.

**Smallest acceptance condition:** For completed contributions, require nonempty completion evidence, a completed/not-applicable due state, and passed/not-required accessibility status. Reconcile commitment status and due state and add negative tests.

### APP-MAT-002 — Approval is not linked to declared practitioner authority

The exact approved draft passed when `decided_by_person_id` was changed to the recipient's person ID rather than the declared practitioner authority. The record proved only that some person record was named.

**Smallest acceptance condition:** Use a stable accountable-human ID in the profile and bind the outreach decision to the governing profile. Validation must require the reviewer to match an authorized approver declaration or delegated-authority record.

### APP-MAT-003 — Portable profiles cannot preserve required capacity and cadence

Adding canonical `capacity` and `review_cadence` fields to the fictional profile failed as unevaluated properties, forcing split authority or generic extensions.

**Smallest acceptance condition:** Add first-class portable fields for capacity and review cadence, or explicitly narrow the contract and define a canonical linked portable record.

### APP-MAT-004 — Placeholder unknowns validate as known values

Changing event accessibility to `"TBD"` passed schema and graph validation, while the structured unknown form also passed.

**Smallest acceptance condition:** Reject defined case-insensitive placeholder sentinels such as `TBD`, `unknown`, and equivalent stand-ins in known branches while retaining structured unknown objects.
