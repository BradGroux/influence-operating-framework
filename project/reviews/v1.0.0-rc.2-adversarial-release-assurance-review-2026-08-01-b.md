## Confirmed findings

### Blocker — restriction precedence is bypassable through stale or removed graph edges

`scripts/validate.py:303-338` derives controlling relationships solely from `participant_ids` and opportunities solely from `contribution.opportunity_ids`; it does not validate reciprocal edges or preserve safety-relevant edges against the baseline.

**Reproduction:** An approved draft passed when:

- the recipient still referenced a do-not-contact relationship but that relationship's participant edge was stale; or
- a do-not-contact opportunity remained intact but its contribution edge was removed.

**Acceptance:** Validate reciprocal graph consistency and baseline preservation of safety-relevant edges; approval must fail closed on contradictory or missing applicable-edge state.

### Material — approval is not bound to profile or record revision

`schemas/common.schema.json:129-139` omits profile/revision identity; `scripts/validate.py:343-360` checks only draft ID, recipient, content, channel, and expiry.

**Reproduction:** Swapping to another authorizing profile and setting `updated_at` after `decided_at` both passed.

**Acceptance:** Bind approval to governing profile and immutable revision/update identity.

### Material — opt-out blocks approval but not draft creation

`docs/09-human-reviewed-outreach.md:23-32` requires stopping before drafting; checks at `scripts/validate.py:282-290` apply only to `approve`.

**Reproduction:** `pending_review` outreach for an opted-out recipient passed.

**Acceptance:** Enforce entry restrictions for draft creation, with an explicit historical-record exception if needed.

### Material — typed graph coverage is incomplete and canonically wrong

`scripts/validate.py:27-46` excludes ecosystem beneficiaries and omits `node_ids`.

**Reproduction:** A canonical ecosystem beneficiary was rejected; an outreach draft used as an ecosystem node passed.

**Acceptance:** Document and enforce every reference union consistently with `CONTEXT.md`.

### Material — structured uncertainty and accountability accept placeholders

`schemas/common.schema.json:98-113,141-154` accepts `TBA` and whitespace-only unknown/decision reasons.

**Acceptance:** Reject common placeholder variants and blank-equivalent strings.

### Material — completed contributions allow impossible dates

`schemas/contribution.schema.json:21-37,59-74` lacks due-window ordering.

**Reproduction:** A completed contribution with `starts_on` after `ends_on` passed.

**Acceptance:** Enforce ordered due dates.

### Material — safety gates miss obvious aliases and leakage locations

`scripts/validate.py:22-25,721-749,787-807` is token- and directory-limited.

**Reproduction:** `dispatch_message: true`, a private email in a root document, and a phone number in public example prose all passed safety validation.

**Acceptance:** Expand semantic capability detection and repository-wide public-data scanning.
