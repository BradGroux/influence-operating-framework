## Executive summary

The corrected framework is canonically coherent. Purpose, Context,
Contribution, Relationship, Judgment, and Learning ask distinct questions and
together cover why to participate, what is known, what value to create, which
continuity and boundaries matter, what an accountable person should decide,
and how outcomes should change the practice.

The seven moves are optional application guidance rather than a mandatory
lifecycle. Governance provides one public authority chain, and accepted
decisions preserve rationale without becoming a competing normative layer. No
reviewed ambiguity is likely to change a normal practitioner decision.

## Coherence confirmations

- **Six concerns:** Their governing questions and glossary definitions remain
  distinct. Context holds decision-relevant evidence and uncertainty, while
  Relationship holds continuity, commitments, consent, and boundaries
  (`framework/operating-framework.md:29-164`;
  `framework/glossary.md:5-99`).
- **Optional method:** The charter rejects a universal sequence, the operating
  framework calls the moves one optional traversal, and the practice method
  permits overlap, repetition, pausing, stopping, and ending without engagement
  (`framework/charter.md:60-92`;
  `framework/operating-framework.md:166-180`;
  `framework/practice-method.md:3-31`).
- **Authority:** The charter and current canonical documents contain normative
  meaning. Governance and the decision index consistently describe accepted
  decisions as rationale implemented through canonical language
  (`GOVERNANCE.md:13-26`; `decisions/README.md:1-16`).
- **Responsible practice:** Material interests require relevant disclosure and
  management; consequential group action requires legitimate authority;
  recipient-set boundaries remain recipient-controlled; and software and AI
  cannot independently choose or transmit external actions
  (`framework/operating-framework.md:119-143`;
  `framework/responsible-practice-standard.md:61-146`).
- **Learning and outcomes:** The framework preserves failure, burden, restraint,
  and uncertainty and assesses operational sustainability separately from
  commercial results (`framework/operating-framework.md:145-164`;
  `framework/measurement-and-learning.md:46-98`).
- **Disposition:** The accepted supplemental corrections appear in canonical
  text, while dissent over the method, engagement guidance, and diagrams remains
  visible without creating conflicting rules
  (`project/reviews/v1.0.0-supplemental-review-disposition-2026-08-01.md:30-110`).
