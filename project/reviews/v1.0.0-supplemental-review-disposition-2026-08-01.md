## Accepted Material findings

1. **Material interests and conflicts:** Accepted from coherence M-01 and misuse
   M-02. The standard now requires relevant disclosure and proportionate
   management; transparency does not cure coercion, missing consent, displaced
   authority, or unmanaged conflict.
2. **Legitimate group authority:** Accepted from coherence M-02 and misuse M-01.
   Consequential group decisions require a named human with a legitimate
   mandate and escalation or narrowing when that authority is absent.
3. **Split public authority:** Accepted from simplicity M-02. Governance now
   provides the single public chain; decisions preserve rationale and have
   normative effect through corresponding canonical language.
4. **Human-versus-tool grammar:** Accepted from simplicity M-04. Accountable
   people own judgment, while software and AI may not independently decide or
   transmit external actions.

## Preserved dissent and rejected scope expansion

### Seven-move method

Simplicity M-01 recommended deleting the practice method as a Material fix. The
recommendation is rejected at that severity. The six concerns define what must
be considered; the seven moves provide one practical way to apply them. The
application reviews found that distinction useful, and both corrected-candidate
reviews found no mandatory-sequence defect. The diagram clarification was
accepted as a Minor improvement.

### Engagement guidance

Simplicity M-03 is partially accepted as simplification and downgraded from
Material. The legal-duty exception was removed and recipient-boundary language
was shortened. Concise direct-communication checks remain because engagement is
part of responsible influence. The corrected adversarial review found the
guidance business-level and free of implementation machinery.

### Mermaid diagrams

Simplicity S-01 is rejected. Three inline source diagrams clarify the concern
relationship, optional method traversal, and framework reading map. There are no
committed generated exports, diagram metadata, Graphviz dependency, or package
dependency. All three render through the lightweight release check.
