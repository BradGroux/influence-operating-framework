# Conference playbook: [conference]

## Intentions

- One to three contribution or learning intentions:
- What would make non-attendance the better choice?
- Capacity and accessibility plan:

## Before

- [ ] Verify current event details and public roles.
- [ ] Prepare useful, accessible artifacts.
- [ ] Check existing relationships, communication boundaries, wait, and do not contact.
- [ ] Obtain bilateral consent for any introduction.
- [ ] Review each draft; approval is not a send.

## During

- [ ] Follow community, recording, photography, and accessibility norms.
- [ ] Prioritize presence and usefulness over contact volume.
- [ ] Record only necessary context with correct privacy.
- [ ] Log promises with owners.

## After

- [ ] Fulfill commitments before new asks.
- [ ] Choose follow-up, wait, no action, or do not contact individually.
- [ ] Reflect on expected versus actual value, burden, and learning.
