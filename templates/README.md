# Templates

Templates help people create compatible, human-readable working records. Adapt headings to the context while preserving the business meaning, evidence, privacy, human decisions, stop conditions, and learning prompts.

Templates are illustrative. The [canonical docs](../docs/README.md) and [schemas](../schemas/README.md) remain the reference for meaning and portable JSON validation.

Use the [practitioner profile](profile.md) to establish mission, goals, capacity, boundaries, ecosystems, channels, measurement priorities, and accountable human authority before starting a cycle.

Use the [research note](research-note.md) to capture source, claim type, confidence, contradiction, staleness, privacy, and human disposition before creating or changing material records.
