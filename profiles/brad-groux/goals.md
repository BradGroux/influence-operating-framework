# Goals

## Initial outcomes

- Increase useful public speaking, workshop, conference, podcast, media, community, and thought-leadership contribution.
- Turn existing modular material into accessible, reusable artifacts matched to real community needs.
- Build relationship continuity through follow-through and contribution rather than activity volume.
- Share successful and failed experiments so others can learn and lead.
- Let aligned Digital Meld opportunities emerge as downstream outcomes.

## Not goals

- Follower or contact volume as the primary measure.
- A database of people to pursue.
- Guaranteed invitations, revenue, or recognition.
- Automated posting, messaging, or follow-up.

## Review questions

What value was created? Who became more capable? Which commitments were honored? What failed? Which public artifact should exist because of the work? What should Brad stop doing?
