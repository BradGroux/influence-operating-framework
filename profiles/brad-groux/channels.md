# Channels

## Supplied strengths and possible channels

- Short talks, conference sessions, and keynotes.
- Modular workshops up to a full day.
- Podcasts, interviews, newsletters, articles, and video.
- Open-source contributions, demos, documentation, and technical guides.
- Community events, user groups, mentoring with consent, and facilitator development.
- AI Dev Days as an education and application anchor.

## Selection rule

Choose channels from verified community need, audience fit, accessibility, evidence, capacity, reuse, and contribution value. Do not default to the largest audience or produce generic submissions merely because material exists.
