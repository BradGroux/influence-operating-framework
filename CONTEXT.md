# Influence Operating Framework

The Influence Operating Framework defines a shared language for ethical influence built through contribution, relationships, reflection, and repeatable practice. It is a single domain context for the initial repository.

## Language

**Influence**:
The capacity to help ideas, people, and communities move through earned trust, useful contribution, and sustained relationships.
_Avoid_: Reach alone, follower count, conversion

**Practitioner**:
The person or mission-led group applying the framework and remaining accountable for its judgments and external actions.
_Avoid_: Seller, account owner

**Ecosystem**:
A connected field of people, organizations, communities, events, channels, projects, and evidence organized around a shared domain or mission.
_Avoid_: Market segment, lead list

**Relationship**:
Continuity and mutual context between people or organizations, supported by evidence and respectful of consent and communication boundaries.
_Avoid_: Prospect, target, conversion opportunity

**Contribution**:
A concrete act or artifact intended to create value for a person, community, or ecosystem without making reciprocity a condition.
_Avoid_: Funnel activity, engagement bait

**Opportunity**:
A possible way to contribute, learn, collaborate, speak, teach, build, or create a legitimate downstream outcome, evaluated with explainable judgment.
_Avoid_: Deal, conversion

**Evidence**:
A cited public or authorized source that supports a fact, inference, hypothesis, decision, or reported outcome while preserving provenance and uncertainty.
_Avoid_: Unattributed intelligence, assumed fact

**Interaction**:
A recorded exchange or shared activity that actually occurred and can be described without inventing familiarity or relationship strength.
_Avoid_: Touch, sales activity

**Outreach draft**:
A proposed external communication that cannot be sent by the framework and must receive an explicit human disposition.
_Avoid_: Automated campaign, send job

**Human disposition**:
The accountable decision to approve, revise, wait, or do not contact after reviewing an outreach draft and its evidence.
_Avoid_: Automated approval

**Do not contact**:
A normal, durable human disposition that prohibits outreach until an accountable human explicitly changes it with documented reason.
_Avoid_: Failed lead, disqualified prospect

**Reflection**:
A source-linked record of what happened, what was learned, and what should change or repeat after an outcome or experiment.
_Avoid_: Performance spin, success story only

**Profile**:
A portable declaration of a practitioner's mission, goals, boundaries, ecosystems, channels, and operating preferences.
_Avoid_: Customer record, buyer persona
