# Learn, document, evolve, repeat

## Trigger and purpose

A success, failure, rejection, contribution, event, outreach outcome, unexpected opportunity, or meaningful no-action decision can improve future practice.

## Inputs and evidence

Expected outcome, actual outcome, source records, commitments, affected people or communities, profile boundary, and privacy classification.

## Flow

1. Record what happened without hindsight rewrite.
2. Separate observation, interpretation, and hypothesis.
3. Describe what worked, failed, surprised, benefited, and burdened.
4. Identify assumptions challenged and artifacts worth creating.
5. Decide whether the lesson is profile-specific or might generalize.
6. Choose repeat, stop, revise, bounded experiment, proposal, or no change.
7. Route canonical or locked changes through governance.
8. Validate the change and begin a new cycle only when mission and capacity justify it.

## Stops and recovery

Stop public synthesis on private or sensitive input, unresolved harm, unsupported causation, absent affected-community perspective, or missing owner. Recover through privacy-safe separation, more evidence, accountable review, or no change.

## Outputs and reflection

Produce a reflection, controlled change or experiment, validation evidence, and intentional repeat or pause. See the [learning-loop visual](../visuals/exports/svg/reflection-learning-loop.svg).
