# Discover, research, and prioritize

## Trigger and purpose

A profile has a bounded ecosystem, event, contribution, or learning question worth investigating.

## Inputs and evidence

Approved profile, public-source boundary, capacity and data budget, prior records, freshness rules, and stop condition.

## Flow

1. Human approves the question and scope.
2. Discovery proposes candidates with source evidence and low or unknown confidence.
3. Human chooses research, reject, no action, or wait.
4. Research labels fact, inference, and hypothesis and preserves contradiction.
5. Verification returns verified, stale, contradicted, uncertain, or rejected claims.
6. Mapping adds only useful evidence-backed nodes and edges.
7. Prioritization compares contribution, learning, capacity, uncertainty, and risk—not people.
8. Human chooses pursue, contribute first, research, wait, decline, no action needed, or do not contact.

## Stops and recovery

Stop on unbounded scope, private source, ambiguous identity, stale consequential claim, unresolved contradiction, privacy concern, or exhausted capacity. Resume only with narrower scope, valid evidence, corrected privacy, or renewed human capacity.

## Outputs and reflection

Produce linked evidence and graph records plus a human decision. Reflect on selection bias, unnecessary data collection, and which signals the method rewarded.
