# Post-event follow-up

## Trigger and purpose

An event ended and actual interactions, contributions, or commitments require accountable disposition.

## Inputs and evidence

Event and interaction IDs, commitments, contribution completion evidence, communication boundaries, wait and do-not-contact states, and human capacity.

## Flow

1. Separate actual interactions from public participant lists or co-presence.
2. Record only necessary, authorized context.
3. Fulfill explicit promises before considering new asks.
4. Share useful artifacts through welcomed channels.
5. Evaluate each possible follow-up independently.
6. Human chooses fulfill, draft, wait, no action needed, or do not contact.
7. Route any draft through the full human-reviewed outreach workflow.
8. Reflect on community value, burden, learning, and changed assumptions.

## Stops and recovery

Stop on missing interaction evidence, silence mistaken for permission, active restriction, unclear promise, privacy concern, or capacity shortfall. Recover by clarifying, fulfilling without an ask, waiting, or closing with no action.

## Outputs and reflection

Produce updated commitments, relationship continuity, dispositions, and reflection—not an automatic cadence.
