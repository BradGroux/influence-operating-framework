# Workflows

Workflows show practical sequences, human gates, stops, recovery, and reflection. They are adaptable paths, not mandatory funnels.

- [Discover, research, and prioritize](discover-research-prioritize.md)
- [Prepare for an event](prepare-for-event.md)
- [Contribution before ask](contribution-before-ask.md)
- [Human-reviewed outreach](human-reviewed-outreach.md)
- [Post-event follow-up](post-event-follow-up.md)
- [Learn, document, evolve, repeat](learn-document-evolve-repeat.md)

No workflow sends external communication. Research may end in no action, wait, or do not contact.
