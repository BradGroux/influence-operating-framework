# Contribution before ask

## Trigger and purpose

Verified evidence suggests a community need that matches practitioner mission and capacity.

## Inputs and evidence

Need evidence, beneficiary and ecosystem IDs, opportunity, community norms, consent dependencies, accessibility, capacity, and maintenance context.

## Flow

1. Verify the need is expressed rather than assumed.
2. Ask whether existing community work should be supported instead.
3. Generate proportionate contribution options, including no action.
4. Human chooses scope, owner, consent path, credit, accessibility, and maintenance.
5. Create and deliver only through approved community channels.
6. Verify value and unintended burden.
7. Evaluate any later ask independently; contribution creates no entitlement.
8. Choose public contribution, human-reviewed outreach, wait, no action, or do not contact.

## Stops and recovery

Stop on unverified need, missing consent, ownership conflict, inaccessible plan, capacity shortfall, unwanted burden, or conditional reciprocity. Recover by narrowing, collaborating, handing off, or declining.

## Outputs and reflection

Produce a contribution record, completion evidence, and reflection. See the [contribution visual](../visuals/exports/svg/contribution-before-ask.svg).
