# Prepare for an event

## Trigger and purpose

A human is evaluating attendance, contribution, submission, teaching, service, or learning at a verified event.

## Inputs and evidence

Event, profile, ecosystem, opportunity, relationship, contribution, and evidence IDs; current organizer sources; capacity, accessibility, safety, cost, and travel boundaries.

## Flow

1. Verify logistics, deadlines, format, organizer, access information, and public roles.
2. Evaluate mission and community fit with narrative and uncertainty.
3. Choose one to three contribution or learning intentions.
4. Distinguish existing relationships from public participant names.
5. Prepare accessible artifacts and obtain consent for introductions.
6. Route any outreach draft through approve, revise, wait, or do not contact.
7. Human decides attend, contribute, submit, wait, decline, or no action.
8. After the event, fulfill promises and reflect before new asks.

## Stops and recovery

Stop on conflicting logistics, inaccessible requirements, capacity or safety concern, absent consent, stale roles, or do-not-contact. Recover through current primary evidence and renewed human decision.

## Outputs and reflection

Produce an event brief, contribution plan, explicit decisions, commitments, and post-event reflection. See the [event workflow visual](../visuals/exports/svg/event-intelligence-workflow.svg).
